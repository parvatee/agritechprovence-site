Multispectral imaging

What the drone sees

Our drone carries two cameras. One takes colour photos, like the human eye. The other measures four bands of light, two of them invisible: red-edge and near infrared.

A healthy leaf absorbs red light for photosynthesis and strongly reflects infrared. A stressed leaf reflects more red and less infrared. The drone measures that change on every vine, across the whole plot, in a single flight.

Our solution

Fly, flag, check

1

Fly

Every plot, every month, at the same time of day and the same height. 2 cm per pixel: every vine is visible.

2

Flag

The camera measures the light reflected by the foliage. Artificial intelligence flags around twenty areas to inspect, ranked, with their GPS coordinates.

3

Check

The winegrower confirms or rules out each alert on the ground. Every check feeds the model, which gets more accurate with each flight.
